**Design Movement:** Organic Modernism — Queensland Coastal Warmth

**Core Principles:**
1. Photography-forward hero sections with bold typographic overlays

- Teal `#3ECFCF` as the dominant action colour — energetic, trustworthy, coastal

- Deep charcoal `#1A2E3B` for headings — grounded, professional
- Warm amber `#F5A623` as a tertiary accent for CTAs

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed hero with diagonal clip-path cut into content below
- Services as horizontal scroll cards on mobile, 3-col asymmetric grid on desktop
- Stats section with large counter numbers in teal on dark background
- Wave SVG dividers between sections to evoke Queensland coastline

**Signature Elements:**
1. Circular photo cutouts with teal ring borders (Alderney-inspired)
2. Diagonal section cuts with 8deg angle
3. Leaf/organic shape SVG motifs in background

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Scroll-triggered fade-up animations for all content blocks
- Counter animations for NDIS stats
- Hover lift effect on service cards (translateY -8px + shadow)

**Animation:**
- Entrance: opacity 0→1 + translateY 40px→0, 600ms ease-out, staggered 80ms
- Counter: 0 to final value over 2s on viewport entry
- Nav: sticky with blur backdrop on scroll

**Typography System:**
- Headings: Playfair Display (serif, editorial weight)
- Body: DM Sans (clean, modern, accessible)
- Accent labels: DM Sans 600 uppercase tracking-widest

## Selected Design: **Organic Modernism — Queensland Coastal Warmth**

This approach best reflects the GHCS brand identity — warm, community-rooted, and professional. The diagonal cuts and wave dividers evoke the Queensland coastline, while the teal + lavender palette directly mirrors the GHCS logo. Photography-forward sections build trust and human connection, and scroll animations create the interactive, engaging experience Abdi requested.
